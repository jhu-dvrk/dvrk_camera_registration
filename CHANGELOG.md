Change log
==========

1.1.0 (TBD)
===========

* Added manual and automatic camera-registration pose collection, with replay
  support for existing pose sets.
* Added safe-pose selection and an angled tracker-mount variant.
* Added ECM and fixed-SUJ support, stereo-calibration checkerboards, and
  visualization enhancements.
* Updated the camera-registration and visualization tools for CRTK and ROS 2.
* Added support for both ROS 1/catkin and ROS 2/colcon builds.
